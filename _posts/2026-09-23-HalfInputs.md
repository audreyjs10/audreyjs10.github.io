This assignment required the code to turn on 4 different LEDs using a simulation of our button and switch commands.

I chose to use the nested conditionals because of the increase in variable required for this code. the number of LED commands would have become very tedious and hard to understand so nesting makes the groups easier to analyze.

A tip I would give is clear naming conventions, especially in codes with a significant amount of repetition.
