This project is three projects rolled into one! The Big Dipper is a light that brightens and then turns off for three seconds. The Flash Bulb
flashes a bright light for half a second every three and a half seconds. Blue Ghost brightens and dims randomly and continuously for the whole duration.


One tip for putting them all together is to use the finder feature to make sure there are no repeating variables and double check the all the numbers align.
