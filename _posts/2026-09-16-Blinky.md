This assignment required programming the Arduino to blink different colors at n second. We had to use IF statements to turn on and off the board at different multiples and second check points.
My tip is to not be afraid to make new lines of code. I kept thinking that making a new line meant i must be doing something wrong, but that always ended up being the right answer.
