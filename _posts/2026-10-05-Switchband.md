This is a parallel circuit integrated into a wrist band. It incorporates a open circuit that is only closed via metal snap to close. There is also a homemade button made out of conductive fabric which is separated by two layers of felt that, when pinched close the circuit and light up the eye of the cat. 

My tip is to return to the paper prototype throughout the sewing stage. I got turned around a few times trying to figure out where I was going with the thread, especially with the snap and two sides of the button. The prototype really helped me to reorient myself.
